Identifying photos:

  1) Plant climbs using tendrils.
     Photo: 1.jpg
     Credits: K. Chayka. Via: https://www.minnesotawildflowers.info/shrub/riverbank-grape

  2) Leaves have long, often reddish petioles.
     Photo: 2.jpg
     Via: https://www.illinoiswildflowers.info

  3) Leaves are lobed and heart-shaped, with coarsely toothed edges.
     Photo: 3.jpg
     Credits: Bruce A. Sorrie. Via: https://namethatplant.net/4DCGI/Query?scientificname=Vitis%20riparia

  4) Fruit grows in branched, drooping clusters.
     Photo: 4.jpg
     Credits: Peter M. Dziuk. Via: https://www.minnesotawildflowers.info/shrub/riverbank-grape

  5) Fruit is dark purple to blue-black with a whitish bloom.
     Photo: 5.jpg
     Credits: Laval University, CC BY-SA 4.0. Via: Wikimedia Commons (no
     specific page given, so this one isn't a clickable link in-game)

  6) Each fruit contains 1–4 small seeds.
     Photo: 6.jpg
     Via: Sheffield's Seeds, https://sheffields.com
